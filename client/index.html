Frequently asked questions

What is Tabsa?

It's a single platform that replaces separate billing machines, order notebooks, and spreadsheets — Tabsa bundles orders, billing, products, inventory, and analytics into one dashboard, whether you run a restaurant, a toy store, an electronics shop, or any other retail business.

Do I need special hardware to use Tabsa?

No special hardware required. Tabsa runs in any modern browser on a tablet, laptop, or desktop at the counter.

Can my whole staff use it at the same time?

Yes — multiple devices can view and update the same tables and orders in real time.

Can I turn off features I don't need?

Yes. Settings lets you show or hide the Inventory and Dashboard modules from the sidebar entirely.

Is there a way to try it before subscribing?

Yes — Get Free Demo opens the live app so you can explore every feature before choosing a plan.
